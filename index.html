What do these mean? (plain-English guide)
Scramble notation

Letters are face turns: R right, L left, U up, D down, F front, B back. A plain letter = clockwise, ' = counter-clockwise, 2 = half turn (180°). A w (e.g. Rw) turns two layers at once (big cubes). Just follow them left to right on a solved cube.

Ao5 & Ao12 (averages)

The speedcuber standard: take your last 5 (or 12) solves, throw out the fastest and the slowest, and average what's left. This stops one lucky or unlucky solve from skewing the number, so it's a fairer picture of your real speed.

PB & Best Ao5/Ao12

PB single is your fastest one solve. Best Ao5/Ao12 is the best rolling average you hit at any point this session — a new PB average is a bigger deal than a single lucky solve.

Consistency (±)

Standard deviation of your times. It says how much your solves bounce around. A low number means you're steady; a high number means some solves are much faster or slower than others.

+2 and DNF

Official penalties. +2 adds two seconds (e.g. the cube was one move from solved) — shown with a +. DNF = Did Not Finish (the solve didn't count). Tap the buttons on any solve to set them.

Inspection & TPS

Inspection is the 15 seconds you're allowed to look before starting (over 15s = +2, over 17s = DNF). TPS = turns per second — type how many moves a solve took and it works out your speed. Higher TPS = faster hands.

Counting turns from the camera

Turn on 🔢 Count turns and the camera counts the bursts of motion during a solve — each face turn makes one spike — and fills in the move count (shown with a ~ because it's an estimate). It can't tell R from U, and very fast hands blur turns together so it under-counts; it's most accurate on steady, learning-pace solves. Tune it with the sensitivity slider, and you can always type the exact number to correct it.

Camera auto-timer

The camera measures how much the picture changes each frame. When it jumps (you grab the cube) the timer starts; when it goes quiet (solved, hands away) it stops. Use the sensitivity slider if it triggers too early or too late. On claude.ai the camera is blocked in artifacts — open this on your local preview to use it.
